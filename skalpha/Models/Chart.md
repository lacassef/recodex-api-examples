# Chart

A **Chart** is the body of `/charts/lua`.

**Where you'll see it:** that route only. `period` is `1D`, `5D`, `1M`, `6M`, `1Y`, `3Y`, `5Y`, `10Y`, `YTD`, or `MAX`. `from` and `to` are `YYYY-MM-DD`.

The body is not a JSON:API document. The guides do not name series keys. Read the keys the response contains.

A `204` has no body.
